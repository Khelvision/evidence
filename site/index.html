The method

What an evidence claim must show

A useful result includes its conditions, costs, rights, and failures. Human interpretation remains separate from what a system observed or calculated.

Real courts

Visible and active games, camera conditions, contamination, omissions, corrections, and failures.

Commercial reality

Licensing across code, models, data, media, APIs, codecs, and outputs—unknown is never green.

Comparable cost

USD and accelerator seconds per source hour, retries, manual minutes, and accepted-output cost.

Owner control

Held-out improvement, export, clean restore, prediction replay, promotion, and rollback.

Human authority

Confirmed facts, model observations, calculations, coach assertions, and unknowns remain visibly distinct.

Reusable tools

Validate, score, compare, and package your own work locally; receive a rationale rather than a leaderboard badge.

Human authority

The coach makes the call

KhelSutra's framework separates confirmed facts, model observations, calculations, coach assertions, and unknowns. KhelVision can help retrieve, measure, clip, assemble, and explain; the coach—or a person coaching themselves—interprets the evidence and decides what happens next.
