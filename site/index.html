Framework preview

There are no official KhelSutra quality releases yet. Checked-in examples are synthetic contract fixtures—not product evidence or a blind benchmark.

What the evidence must show

Real courts

Visible and active games, camera conditions, contamination, omissions, corrections, and failures.

Commercial reality

Licensing across code, models, data, media, APIs, codecs, and outputs—unknown is never green.

Comparable cost

USD and accelerator seconds per source hour, retries, manual minutes, and accepted-output cost.

Owner control

Held-out improvement, export, clean restore, prediction replay, promotion, and rollback.

Human authority

Confirmed facts, model observations, calculations, coach assertions, and unknowns remain visibly distinct.

Reusable tools

Validate, score, compare, and package your own work locally; receive a rationale rather than a leaderboard badge.

The boundary

KhelSutra is an AI agent for the coach. It is never the AI coach. It retrieves, measures, clips, assembles, and explains. The coach—or a person coaching themselves—interprets the evidence and decides what happens next.
